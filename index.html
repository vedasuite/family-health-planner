Family history

Add parents, brothers, sisters and children first. These count most. Grandparents, uncles and aunts are noted but do not change the scores. For a heart attack, blockage, bypass or stroke, add the age it first happened. For a cancer, add the age it was found. If a relative has their own saved profile on this device, link it so you enter their details only once.

Results also update by themselves as you type. On a phone they appear below this form.

Emergency health card

For you, or for a parent you look after. Fill it once, keep a copy on the phone and a printed one in the wallet or on the fridge. It stays on this device. Only what you copy or print leaves it. Do not enter bank, card or Aadhaar numbers.

Privacy and your data
  • There is no account and no server that stores what you type. Saved profiles and the emergency card are kept in this browser, on this device only. Anyone who uses the same browser can open them.
  • Nothing leaves your device unless you press Copy, Print, Invite or a WhatsApp button. On the Claude version, pressing the explain button sends your results summary to Claude.
  • Like any website, the web host keeps ordinary access logs, such as your IP address. This page has no analytics and no ads.
  • Health details are sensitive. If this ever becomes a service that stores data, it will need your consent and a privacy policy under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act.
