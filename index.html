How it works

Three easy steps. About five minutes.

No sign-up and nothing to download. You can stop at any step and still get useful answers.

  1. Tell us about you

    Your age, whether you are male or female, your habits, and any illness you already have. If you have a recent lab report, you can add the numbers too.

  2. Add your family

    Who in your family had diabetes, heart problems, cancer or kidney problems, and at what age it started. That age matters a lot.

  3. Get your plan

    See what to watch for, which tests to ask for first, and how to plan the cost. Take the summary to your doctor.

Why trust it

Honest about what it is, and what it is not.

A health tool should show how it works. Here is how ours does.

What it does

  • Uses fixed, written rules. No AI decides your risk.
  • Shows where every point comes from, with Indian guidelines first.
  • Keeps what you type on your own device. You can delete it any time.
  • Lists every rule openly, so a doctor can check it.

What it will not do

  • Diagnose any illness, or tell you to start or stop a medicine.
  • Replace a doctor. A "lower" result does not mean you are fully healthy.
  • Claim that a doctor has approved it. That check has not happened yet.
  • Cover children, pregnancy, vaccines or mental health in detail.

Find care and support

If you do not have a doctor yet, start here. These are public services in India. Please check the timings and whether they are available in your area.

Free doctor consultation by phone or video

eSanjeevani, the Health Ministry's telemedicine service. esanjeevani.mohfw.gov.in

Free screening near you

Ask at your nearest Ayushman Arogya Mandir or primary health centre. The national programme checks blood pressure, sugar, and mouth, breast and cervical cancer from age 30.

Stress, low mood, sleep, anxiety

Tele-MANAS, free and 24 hours, in many languages: 14416 or 1800-89-14416. It offers first-level counselling and is not for emergencies.

Emergency

Call 112 (national emergency number). 108 is the ambulance number in most states. Chest pain, signs of a stroke, heavy bleeding or severe breathlessness are emergencies. Do not wait for an appointment.

Health ID (ABHA)

A free number that keeps your reports and prescriptions together across hospitals. abha.abdm.gov.in

Government insurance hospitals

If you qualify for Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY), find the hospitals that accept it here: hospitals.pmjay.gov.in

Quality labs

NABL approves good medical labs. nabl-india.org has a list. Ask for the package price before you book.

Check a doctor's registration

Every registered doctor is listed in the National Medical Commission's Indian Medical Register. nmc.org.in

Which doctor for what

Diabetes, blood pressure, cholesterol, thyroid, anaemia, liverStart with a general physician or family doctor. They refer to a diabetologist, endocrinologist, cardiologist or gastroenterologist if needed.
Heart and strokePhysician first. Cardiologist for chest symptoms, a past event, or a strong family history. Neurologist after a stroke.
KidneyPhysician first, then a kidney specialist (nephrologist) if kidney test results are low or the urine shows protein.
Prostate and urinaryUrologist (specialist for the urine system and prostate). A physician can order the first PSA and urine tests.
Cancer in the familyPhysician, then a cancer specialist (oncologist) or a genetic counsellor if the pattern in the family is strong.
Cancer screening from 30Ayushman Arogya Mandir, primary health centre or a gynaecologist for breast and cervical checks.
Move my saved profiles to another phone or computer

There is no account, so your profiles stay on one device. To move them, make a backup code here and paste it on the other device. The code holds the health details you saved, and anyone can read it, so send it only to yourself or to someone you trust.

Privacy and your data
  • There is no account, and no server stores what you type. Saved profiles and the emergency card stay in this browser, on this device only. Anyone who uses the same browser can open them.
  • Nothing leaves your device unless you press Copy, Print, Invite or a WhatsApp button. On the Claude version, pressing the Explain button sends your results summary to Claude.
  • Like any website, the web host keeps basic visit records, such as your IP address. This page has no tracking and no ads.
  • Health details are private. If this ever starts storing data on a server, it will first ask for your consent and follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act.
