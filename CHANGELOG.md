# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - 13 Nov 2026
- New soft violet look with a short Home page and a separate Check my family page.
- Plainer words everywhere: medical terms are explained and test names say what they check.
- No medical rule, number or source was changed.

## 0.8.0 – 13 Nov 2026
- Redesign: warm off-white palette, deep teal actions, serif headings (Fraunces) with Plus Jakarta Sans body, glass sticky navigation, hero with honest trust points, bento overview, how-it-works, trust section, reassuring footer, hover and focus polish. No medical rule changed.

## 0.7.0 – 13 Nov 2026
- Prostate: Urological Society of India 2022 guideline is now the first source (PSA 4 as a point for further evaluation, after 50 for men at risk); ACS kept for schedules.
- Anaemia: haemoglobin cut-offs and severity bands from Anemia Mukt Bharat; severe (below 8) says see a doctor; yearly check for women 20 to 49.
- Thyroid: ICMR hypothyroidism workflow (TSH with free T4, reasons to treat subclinical).
- Colon: family-history start age and 5-year colonoscopy (US Multi-Society Task Force); states that India has no national colon programme; USPSTF 45 to 75 for average risk.
- Insurance now follows the IRDAI Master Circular of 29 May 2024 (36-month cap, 60-month moratorium, portability, 1-hour cashless decision).
- Checks on typed values (swapped BP, mmol/L LDL, micromol/L creatinine, implausible numbers).
- Report reader: paste report text, values are found on the device and confirmed by the user.
- Backup code to move saved profiles between devices.
- Find care and support: eSanjeevani, Tele-MANAS, emergency numbers, ABHA, PM-JAY hospital finder, NABL, which doctor for what.
- Privacy and terms page, Hindi guide, rules register (RULES.md), review sheet now 25 rules and 34 scenarios.
