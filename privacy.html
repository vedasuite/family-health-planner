Privacy and terms

Draft of 13 Nov 2026. This is a plain-language draft written for a test version. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer and should be before the tool is promoted to the public.

What this tool is

Family Health Planner is a free awareness tool. You enter your age, habits, conditions and family history, and it shows what to be aware of, which tests to ask a doctor about, and how to plan for costs. It uses fixed rules written from published guidelines. It does not diagnose anything and it is not medical advice.

What happens to your information

  • We do not collect it. There are no accounts, no server that stores what you type, no analytics, no ads and no tracking cookies.
  • It stays on your device. If you press Save, your profile, the emergency card and the family details are kept in your browser's local storage on that device. Anyone who can open the same browser can see them. The "Delete everything saved on this device" button removes them.
  • The report reader works on your device. Pasted report text is read by code in your browser and is not sent anywhere.
  • Backup codes are made on your device. A code contains the health details you saved in a form anyone can decode, so share it only with yourself or someone you trust.

When information leaves your device

Only when you choose to: pressing Copy, Print, Invite, a WhatsApp button, the calendar file, or a language link. Then the destination (your clipboard, a printer, WhatsApp, Google Translate) handles it under its own policy. The language links send the page address to Google Translate, not your saved details. On the Claude version of the page, pressing Explain sends your results summary to Claude.

The hosting provider (Vercel) and the code host (GitHub) keep ordinary technical logs, such as IP address and time of visit, as any website does.

Health data is sensitive

Because it is sensitive, the tool is built so that nothing is stored on a server. India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and its Rules (notified in November 2025, with obligations phased in over about 18 months) apply to organisations that process personal data. If this tool ever starts to store data on a server, it will need a proper notice, your consent, a way to withdraw it and a way to delete your data before it does.

Terms of use

  • Use it for awareness only. It cannot tell you that you do or do not have a condition. A normal result does not mean you are healthy, and a high one does not mean you are ill.
  • Do not use it in an emergency. Call 112, or 108 for an ambulance in most states, or go to the nearest hospital.
  • Do not change or stop medicines because of this tool. Talk to your doctor.
  • The rules come from published guidelines and some are simple groupings made for this prototype. They have not yet been reviewed by a doctor, and guidelines change. The page shows the version and the date the sources were last checked.
  • The tool is provided as it is, without any promise that it is complete or error-free. To the extent the law allows, the maker is not responsible for decisions taken from it.
  • It is for adults. It is not meant for children or for pregnancy.
  • Do not enter bank, card or Aadhaar numbers anywhere in the tool.
