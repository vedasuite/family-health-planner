Review sheet for doctors

Family Health Planner, rules updated 13 Nov 2026. This page lists every rule the tool uses and what it answers for 34 example families.

What we are asking. The tool is a screening-awareness aid, not a diagnosis. Please tell us where a rule or an answer is wrong, unsafe, too cautious or missing. About 20 to 30 minutes. Nothing here is sent anywhere. At the end you copy your review and send it back by email or WhatsApp.

1. The rules

“Prototype rule” means we wrote it ourselves and it has not been validated. Those matter most for your review.
